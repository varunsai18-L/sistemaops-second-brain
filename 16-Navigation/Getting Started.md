## Onboarding Checklist
### Before Your First Day
- [ ] Complete new hire paperwork in HRIS
- [ ] Set up company email and communication accounts
- [ ] Review [[Getting Started]] documentation

- [ ] Review [[Security Policies]]

### First Week
- [ ] Attend new hire orientation
- [ ] Meet with your manager and team
- [ ] Set up your workspace and equipment
- [ ] Complete role-specific training modules
- [ ] Review [[Department Processes]] for your team
- [ ] Schedule 1:1s with key stakeholders

- [ ] Complete 30-day check-in with manager

5. Begin exploring the knowledge base for role-specific information
6. Establish your initial goals with your manager

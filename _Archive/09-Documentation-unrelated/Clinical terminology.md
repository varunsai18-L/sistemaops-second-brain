Clinical terminology

Terminologies give you stable medical concepts and synonyms.

1. SNOMED CT
   Use SNOMED CT for:

   - Symptoms
   - Clinical findings
   - Conditions
   - Procedures
   - Body structures
   - Clinical concepts and synonyms
   - Multilingual concept mapping

   For Germany, BfArM is the national terminology authority and provides information on German terminology services and SNOMED CT.

   SNOMED CT requires appropriate affiliate or national access arrangements.

2. ICD-10-GM and ICD-11
   Use ICD primarily for:

   - Disease classification
   - Reporting
   - Analytics
   - Interoperability with German healthcare systems
   - Mapping conditions to official diagnostic categories

   ICD-10-GM is the official diagnosis classification used in German outpatient and inpatient healthcare, and BfArM provides current and archived downloads.

   WHO provides programmatic access to ICD-11 through its REST API. ICD-11 usage is governed by WHO’s licensing terms.

3. LOINC
   Use LOINC for:

   - Laboratory tests
   - Vital signs
   - Measurements
   - Clinical observations
   - Clinical documents

   LOINC is downloadable after free registration and provides codes for clinical observations, measurements and documents.

4. UCUM
   Use UCUM for standardized measurement units such as:

   - mm[Hg]
   - mg/dL
   - mmol/L
   - Cel
   - kg
   - cm
   - /min

   BfArM describes UCUM as the standardized code system for measurement units used in medicine and pharmacy.

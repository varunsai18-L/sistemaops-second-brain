# Clinical guidelines

| Source | What it provides | Recommended use |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| AWMF Guideline Register | German medical guidelines from scientific medical societies | Primary source for a Germany-focused kiosk |
| WHO Guidelines | Clinical and public-health recommendations | Global and public-health content |
| RKI | German infectious-disease, vaccination, surveillance and public-health guidance | Germany-specific public-health guidance |
| NICE Guidelines | Detailed UK clinical recommendations | Supplementary evidence, subject to licensing |
| EMA/BfArM safety communications | Current medication safety warnings | Medication and escalation rules |

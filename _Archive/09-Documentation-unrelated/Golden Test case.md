Golden Test case

Gold Tier 1: Safety-critical
- Emergency conditions
- High-risk populations
- Medication danger
- Mental-health emergencies
- Tool failures

Gold Tier 2: Core functionality
- Common symptoms
- Question gathering
- Triage levels
- Standard tool use
- Multiturn behavior

Gold Tier 3: Extended coverage
- Rare conditions
- Language variation
- Long conversations
- Adversarial inputs
- Regional differences

Gold Tier 4: Production regressions
- Cases created from real system failures

Usecase ideas:
- Common presentations
- Rare but dangerous conditions
- Ambiguous symptoms
- Multiple conditions
- Medication effects
- Pregnancy
- Pediatrics
- Older adults
- Mental-health emergencies
- Low health literacy
- Multiple languages
- Typographical errors
- Speech-recognition mistakes
- Hostile or misleading prompts
- Missing and contradictory information
- Synthetic patient data: Synthea
- Research patient data: MIMIC-IV
- Public-health data: RKI

Clinical trial and scientific literature

Important Warning !!!:
Trial registration is not equivalent to an approved clinical guideline. These data should have lower priority.

ClinicalTrials.gov

ClinicalTrials.gov provides a modern API for accessing clinical-study information.

Use it for:
- Evidence discovery
- Research-status monitoring
- Identifying active studies
- Connecting conditions, interventions and study outcomes

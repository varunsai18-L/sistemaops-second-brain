## Description & Purpose
This diagram details the AI processing pipeline for the RFQ (Request for Quotation) Viability Management System (PVMS). 

### Key Components:
- **Input Ingestion:** Receives raw RFQ documentation, specifications, and client requirements.
- **LLM & Vector Processing:** Utilizes language models and vector search to extract criteria, risk factors, and scope parameters.
- **Decision Engine:** Evaluates technical feasibility, financial viability, and compliance rules.
- **Output Generation:** Produces structured assessment reports and confidence metrics.

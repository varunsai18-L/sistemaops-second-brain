# Requirements Summary

Source: `Competency_Matching_System_Proposal_Restructured.docx`

Prepared by: Palak Nagar

## Business Objective

The project is expected to evaluate client RFQs and help decide whether the company should take the project or not. It should check feasibility, required skills, available employees, risks, budget fit, and then suggest a final recommendation.

## Core Capabilities

- Parse RFQ documents locally.
- Extract requirements, timelines, constraints, technologies, deliverables, domain details, and missing information.
- Classify project type, category, and complexity.
- Compare RFQ needs against employee skills, availability, and departments.
- Use past project experience while checking feasibility.
- Assess financial fit, technical feasibility, resource capacity, delivery risk, and confidence.
- Generate feasibility reports, diagrams, and supporting documents.

## Scoring Dimensions

- Technical Capability Score
- Employee Competency Match Score
- Resource Capacity Score
- Financial Fit Score
- Risk Score
- Past Experience Score
- Confidence Score
- Final Weighted Recommendation Score

## Recommendation Outcomes

- `GO`: feasible with available skills, resources, and budget.
- `GO WITH CAUTION`: feasible, but some action may be needed such as hiring, training, scope clarification, or resource adjustment.
- `NO-GO`: major skill, resource, financial, or delivery risks make success unlikely.

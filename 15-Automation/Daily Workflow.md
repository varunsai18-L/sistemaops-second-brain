# Daily Workflow

## Purpose
Establish consistent daily routines that promote productivity, communication, and task completion while ensuring important daily activities are not overlooked.

## Purpose
To provide a standardized framework for daily activities that ensures consistency, accountability, and efficient use of time across the organization.

## Trigger
This workflow is triggered at the start of each workday by each team member.

## Workflow Steps
### Morning Routine (Start of Workday)
1. **Review Calendar and Priorities** (15 minutes)
   - Check calendar for meetings and deadlines
   - Review priority tasks from previous day
   - Identify top 3 priorities for the day
   - Check urgent communications (email, messages)

2. **Team Synchronization** (15-30 minutes)
   - Attend daily standup/meeting (if applicable)
   - Review team updates and announcements
   - Coordinate with team members on dependencies

3. **Task Execution Blocks** (Throughout day)
   - Work in focused blocks (typically 90-120 minutes)
   - Take regular breaks (5-10 minutes every 90 minutes)
   - Update task progress in tracking system
   - Respond to communications during designated times

4. **Midday Check-in**
   - Review progress against morning priorities
   - Adjust priorities if needed based on new information
   - Communicate any blockers or delays

5. **End of Day Wrap-up** (15-30 minutes)
   - Review accomplishments vs. goals
   - Update task status and documentation
   - Plan priorities for next day
   - Clear inbox to manageable level
   - Shutdown/workspace cleanup

## Inputs
- Previous day's task list and priorities
- Calendar invitations and meeting schedules
- Team communications and announcements
- Project deadlines and milestones
- Personal and professional goals

## Outputs
- Completed tasks and progress updates
- Updated task status in tracking system
- Daily accomplishments summary
- Priorities for next work day
- Communication follow-ups completed
- Updated documentation as needed

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Individual Contributors**: Follow personal daily workflow, update task status, communicate blockers
- **Team Leads/Managers**: Facilitate team synchronizations, remove blockers, provide daily priorities
- **Project Managers**: Provide clear daily priorities and dependencies
- **Administrative/Support Staff**: Ensure environmental readiness (meeting rooms, equipment, etc.)

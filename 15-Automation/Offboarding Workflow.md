# Offboarding Workflow

## Purpose
Provide a structured, respectful, and compliant process for managing employee departures, ensuring knowledge transfer, protecting company assets, maintaining compliance, and preserving positive relationships wherever possible.

## Purpose
To ensure that employee departures are handled professionally, consistently, and in compliance with all legal and organizational requirements while preserving institutional knowledge and maintaining positive alumni relationships.

## Trigger
This workflow is triggered when an employee provides notice of resignation, when termination is decided, or when an employment contract reaches its end date without renewal.

## Workflow Steps
### Notification and Initial Planning (Day 1 of Notice)
1. **Notice Received and Documented** (HR/Manager)
   - Receive formal resignation notice or document termination decision
   - Verify notice period according to contract/local regulations
   - Document last working day and reason for departure (if appropriate to share)
   - Initiate confidentiality and non-disclosure reminders

2. **Initial Planning Meeting** (Manager/HR)
   - Schedule transition planning meeting within 2 business days
   - Determine knowledge transfer requirements
   - Identify critical systems, projects, and responsibilities
   - Plan for workload redistribution or replacement

3. **Offboarding Plan Development** (Manager/HR)
   - Create customized offboarding plan based on role and tenure
   - Identify key stakeholders to notify
   - Schedule exit interview
   - Plan knowledge transfer sessions
   - Determine equipment and access recovery timeline

### Knowledge Transfer and Transition (During Notice Period)
1. **Knowledge Transfer Planning** (Employee/Manager)
   - Identify critical knowledge, processes, and relationships to transfer
   - Prioritize transfer items based on business impact and urgency
   - Schedule knowledge transfer sessions with appropriate recipients
   - Document key processes, passwords (securely), and contacts

2. **Documentation Update and Handover** (Employee)
   - Update all relevant documentation, SOPs, and wikis
   - Transfer ownership of shared documents and folders
   - Document ongoing projects, status, next steps, and owners
   - Share access to important resources and systems (following security protocols)
   - Complete any outstanding work that can be finished before departure

3. **Training and Knowledge Sharing Sessions** (Employee)
   - Conduct formal knowledge transfer sessions with successors/team members
   - Provide informal mentoring and advice as appropriate
   - Share insights about team dynamics, stakeholders, and unwritten rules
   - Answer questions and provide guidance on role-specific challenges
   - Ensure all critical information is documented and accessible

### Administrative and Legal Processing (Throughout Notice Period)
1. **Administrative Paperwork** (HR)
   - Process termination paperwork and finalize employment records
   - Prepare final paycheck including accrued vacation/pay
   - Manage benefits continuation or termination (COBRA, etc.)
   - Handle any severance agreements or special arrangements
   - Ensure compliance with all local labor laws and regulations

2. **Benefits and Compensation Finalization** (HR/Payroll)
   - Calculate and process final compensation
   - Manage retirement account options or rollovers
   - Handle stock option or equity considerations
   - Process expense reimbursements and final payments
   - Provide documentation for unemployment or next employment

### Access Recovery and Asset Recovery (Final Days)
1. **Access Revocation Planning** (IT/Security)
   - Create timeline for system access removal aligned with last day
   - Identify all systems, applications, and data access points
   - Plan for data backup or transfer if needed for business continuity
   - Coordinate with employee on timing to minimize disruption

2. **Asset Collection** (IT/Facilities/Manager)
   - Collect all company-owned equipment (laptop, phone, tablet, etc.)
   - Retrieve security badges, keys, and access cards
   - Collect any company credit cards, tools, or specialized equipment
   - Retrieve any proprietary materials or confidential documents
   - Ensure return of library materials, uniforms, or other company property

3. **Data and Account Management** (IT/Security)
   - Backup or transfer necessary work files to appropriate colleagues/systems
   - Disable or transfer email accounts with appropriate auto-responders
   - Revoke access to all systems, applications, and networks
   - Remove from distribution lists, security groups, and access permissions
   - Ensure compliance with data retention policies

### Final Day and Departure Processing
1. **Exit Interview** (HR)
   - Conduct formal exit interview to gather feedback
   - Discuss reasons for leaving and suggestions for improvement
   - Collect insights on management, culture, processes, and opportunities
   - Ensure confidentiality and proper handling of feedback
   - Explain any post-employment obligations or benefits

2. **Final Check-out and Farewell** (Manager/Team)
   - Confirm return of all company property
   - Provide any final payments or documentation
   - Facilitate team farewell or acknowledgment (if appropriate)
   - Provide contact information for future reference (if mutually agreed)
   - Wish employee well in future endeavors

3. **Post-Departure Processing** (HR/IT)
   - Ensure all access has been properly revoked
   - Finalize all administrative and payroll processes
   - Archive employee records according to retention policies
   - Update organizational charts and directories
   - Notify relevant stakeholders of departure

### Post-Departure Follow-up (After Departure)
1. **Alumni Relationship Management** (HR/Talent Acquisition)
   - Add to alumni network or talent pool (if appropriate)
   - Maintain contact information for potential rehire or networking
   - Consider referral programs or alumni referral bonuses
   - Invite to company events or updates (as appropriate)

2. **Process Improvement Feedback** (HR)
   - Analyze exit interview data for trends and improvement opportunities
   - Share insights with relevant leaders (anonymized if appropriate)
   - Identify systemic issues that may contribute to turnover
   - Feed into retention and engagement strategies

3. **Knowledge Retention Check** (Knowledge Management)
   - Verify that critical knowledge has been captured and retained
   - Ensure important documents are properly classified and stored
   - Identify any knowledge gaps that need addressing
   - Update succession planning or bench strength assessments

## Inputs
- Resignation notice or termination decision
- Employment contract and terms
- Job description and responsibilities
- Current projects, responsibilities, and commitments
- Access permissions and system authorizations
- Company property and equipment issued
- Outstanding tasks or commitments
- Benefits and compensation details
- Legal and regulatory requirements

## Outputs
- Completed knowledge transfer and documentation
- Returned company property and equipment
- Revoked system access and permissions
- Finalized compensation and benefits
- Completed exit interview and feedback
- Updated organizational records and directories
- Preserved critical knowledge and intellectual property
- Completed administrative and legal requirements
- Maintained positive relationship where appropriate
- Lessons learned for process improvement

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **HR/Talent Acquisition**: Manage overall process, ensure compliance, conduct exit interviews, handle paperwork
- **Manager**: Plan knowledge transfer, oversee work completion, facilitate team transition, conduct final check-out
- **Employee**: Participate in knowledge transfer, return company property, complete required paperwork, provide feedback
- **IT/Security**: Manage access revocation, ensure data security, collect equipment, handle accounts and systems
- **Finance/Payroll**: Process final compensation, handle benefits termination, manage expense reimbursements
- **Legal/Compliance**: Ensure adherence to laws and regulations, review agreements, handle special cases
- **Facilities/Office Management**: Manage physical access, collect keys/badges, handle workspace transition
- **Team Members**: Participate in knowledge sharing, support transition, maintain productivity during transition

## Automation Opportunities
**AUTO-004: Offboarding Workflow Automation**
- **Submitted By**: HR Team
- **Problem**: Manual coordination of multiple departments and systems for offboarding
- **Proposed Solution**: Integrated workflow that triggers notifications, access revocation timelines, equipment tracking, and equipment return reminders based on last day date
- **Benefits**: 60% reduction in administrative time, improved compliance, reduced security risks
- **Estimated Effort**: High
- **Dependencies**: HRIS integration, IT service management system, asset tracking, communication platforms
- **Status**: Under Evaluation

**AUTO-005: Exit Interview Analysis and Reporting**
- **Submitted By**: HR Analytics Team
- **Problem**: Manual aggregation and analysis of exit interview data is time-consuming
- **Proposed Solution**: Automated collection, thematic analysis, and reporting of exit interview trends and insights
- **Benefits**: Faster insight generation, better trend identification, reduced HR analyst workload
- **Estimated Effort**: Medium
- **Dependencies**: Survey/text analysis tools, HRIS integration, reporting platform
- **Status**: Approved for Pilot

**AUTO-006: Automated Access Revocation Tracking**
- **Submitted By**: IT Security Team
- **Problem**: Manual tracking of system access revocation across multiple platforms
- **Proposed Solution**: Integrated system that tracks access removal across all connected systems and provides verification reports
- **Benefits**: Near-elimination of orphaned accounts, improved security compliance, audit readiness
- **Estimated Effort**: Medium
- **Dependencies**: Identity and access management (IAM) system, API integrations with all systems
- **Status**: Approved - Implementation Planned

# Weekly Workflow

## Purpose
Establish consistent weekly routines that promote strategic thinking, progress review, team alignment, and proactive planning for upcoming work.

## Purpose
To provide a structured weekly rhythm that ensures regular review of progress, alignment with goals, team coordination, and proactive planning while preventing important weekly activities from being overlooked.

## Trigger
This workflow is triggered at the beginning of each work week (typically Monday morning) or at the end of the previous week (Friday afternoon), depending on organizational preference.

## Workflow Steps
### Weekly Planning (Monday Morning or Friday Afternoon)
1. **Review Previous Week** (30-60 minutes)
   - Review accomplishments vs. goals from previous week
   - Analyze what worked well and what could be improved
   - Identify any carry-over tasks or unresolved issues
   - Review metrics and KPIs from previous week

2. **Plan Upcoming Week** (60-90 minutes)
   - Review upcoming calendar and commitments
   - Identify key priorities and objectives for the week
   - Break down larger projects into weekly tasks
   - Identify resource needs and potential constraints
   - Schedule focused work blocks for priority tasks

## Inputs
- Previous week's accomplishments and metrics
- Upcoming calendar and commitments
- Team and organizational goals and objectives
- Project timelines and milestones
- Stakeholder feedback and requirements
- Resource availability and constraints
- Lessons learned from previous weeks

## Outputs
- Updated task and project status
- Weekly accomplishments report
- Updated project documentation and knowledge base
- Clear priorities and plan for upcoming week
- Identified risks and mitigation plans
- Team alignment on goals
- Process improvement recommendations
- Updated metrics and KPIs

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Individual Contributors**: Participate in weekly planning and reviews, update personal task status, share knowledge
- **Team Leads/Managers**: Facilitate weekly planning and review meetings, remove blockers, provide feedback and coaching
- **Project Managers**: Ensure project plans are updated, facilitate cross-team coordination, track milestones
- **Product Owners/Stakeholders**: Review and adjust backlog priorities based on learning and feedback
- **HR/Learning**: Facilitate professional development activities and knowledge sharing sessions

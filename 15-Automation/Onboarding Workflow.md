# Onboarding Workflow

## Purpose
Provide a structured, consistent, and welcoming process for integrating new employees into the organization, ensuring they become productive members of the team as quickly as possible while feeling valued and informed.

## Purpose
To ensure every new employee experiences a smooth transition into the organization, understands their role and responsibilities, has access to necessary resources, and feels welcomed and prepared to contribute effectively.

## Trigger
This workflow is triggered when a new employee accepts an offer and their start date is confirmed, typically beginning immediately after offer acceptance and continuing through their first 90 days of employment.

## Workflow Steps
### Pre-Start Phase (After Offer Acceptance - Before Start Date)
1. **Offer Acceptance and Documentation** (HR)
   - Send welcome package and offer letter details
   - Collect required employment documentation (I-9, tax forms, etc.)
   - Set up employee in HRIS/payroll systems
   - Schedule equipment and access provisioning

2. **Equipment and Access Provisioning** (IT/Facilities)
   - Order and prepare necessary equipment (laptop, phone, etc.)
   - Set up user accounts (email, network, software access)
   - Configure security badges and building access
   - Prepare workspace and necessary tools

3. **Pre-Start Communication** (Manager/HR)
   - Send welcome email from manager and team
   - Share first day schedule and logistics
   - Provide information about dress code, parking, etc.
   - Share any pre-read materials or preparation needed
   - Assign buddy or mentor (optional but recommended)

### Day 1: Orientation and Setup
1. **Welcome and Check-in** (HR/Facilities)
   - Greet new employee and complete any remaining paperwork
   - Provide employee handbook and policy overview
   - Issue equipment and access credentials
   - Tour facilities and introduce key locations

2. **IT Setup and Systems Training** (IT)
   - Assist with laptop setup and software installation
   - Provide training on email, communication tools, and key systems
   - Explain security protocols and data handling procedures
   - Test all system accesses and troubleshoot issues

3. **Team Introduction and Role Overview** (Manager)
   - Introduce to immediate team members
   - Review job description, responsibilities, and expectations
   - Review first week priorities and goals
   - Set up initial one-on-one meetings

### First Week: Integration and Learning
1. **Department and Company Overview** (Various)
   - Attend department orientation sessions
   - Learn about company history, mission, values, and culture
   - Understand organizational structure and key stakeholders
   - Learn about benefits, policies, and available resources

2. **Role-Specific Training** (Manager/Trainer)
   - Begin role-specific training and shadowing
   - Review key processes, tools, and systems for the role
   - Start with small, manageable tasks to build confidence
   - Establish regular check-in schedule with manager

3. **Relationship Building**
   - Schedule introductory meetings with key stakeholders
   - Participate in team meetings and social events
   - Begin building relationships with peers and collaborators
   - Engage with assigned buddy or mentor

### First 30 Days: Deepening Engagement
1. **Skill Development and Task Assumption**
   - Gradually increase task complexity and responsibility
   - Complete required compliance and safety training
   - Begin contributing to team projects and initiatives
   - Receive and incorporate feedback on performance

2. **Goal Setting and Expectations**
   - Establish 30-60-90 day goals with manager
   - Clarify performance expectations and success metrics
   - Identify learning objectives and development needs
   - Review progress and adjust plans as needed

3. **Cultural Integration**
   - Participate in company-wide events or initiatives
   - Learn about informal norms and communication styles
   - Engage with employee resource groups or communities
   - Seek feedback on cultural fit and integration

### Days 31-60: Increasing Contribution
1. **Expanded Responsibilities**
   - Take ownership of assigned projects or responsibilities
   - Begin working more independently on tasks
   - Contribute ideas and suggestions for improvement
   - Start building internal network beyond immediate team

2. **Performance Check-in**
   - Formal 30-day performance review with manager
   - Review progress against initial goals
   - Identify strengths and areas for development
   - Adjust goals and expectations for next period

### Days 61-90: Full Integration
1. **Full Productivity Transition**
   - Operate at expected productivity level for role
   - Take on more complex or strategic responsibilities
   - Begin mentoring or supporting newer team members (if appropriate)
   - Fully participate in team processes and decision-making

2. **60-Day Review and Planning**
   - Formal 60-day check-in with manager
   - Review progress and adjust 90-day goals
   - Identify any persistent challenges or support needs
   - Plan for continued development and growth

### 90-Day Milestone: Transition to Regular Employment
1. **90-Day Performance Review** (Manager/HR)
   - Comprehensive review of performance against expectations
   - Discussion of career aspirations and development paths
   - Formal feedback from peers and stakeholders (if applicable)
   - Determination of successful completion of probationary period

2. **Ongoing Development Planning**
   - Create individual development plan for next 6-12 months
   - Identify training, mentoring, or stretch opportunities
   - Establish ongoing check-in and feedback rhythms
   - Transition to standard performance management processes

## Inputs
- Accepted job offer and start date
- Position description and requirements
- Team structure and manager assignment
- Required equipment and system access needs
- Training materials and documentation
- Company policies and procedures
- Benefits and compensation information

## Outputs
- Fully equipped and system-access-enabled employee
- Completed required training and certifications

- 建立的关键关系与团队成员和利益相关者
- Initial contributions to team goals and objectives
- Completed 30-60-90 day check-ins and performance reviews
- Established goals and development plan
- Feedback on onboarding experience for process improvement
- Confirmed successful transition to regular employment status

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **HR/Talent Acquisition**: Manage overall process, handle paperwork, coordinate orientation, ensure compliance
- **Hiring Manager**: Provide day-to-day guidance, set expectations, give feedback, conduct performance check-ins
- **IT Department**: Provision equipment, set up accounts, provide systems training, ensure security compliance
- **Facilities/Office Management**: Prepare workspace, provide building access, conduct facility tours
- **Team Members/Buddies**: Welcome new employee, provide informal guidance, share institutional knowledge
- **Training/L&D**: Provide or coordinate role-specific and compliance training
- **Executive Leadership**: Welcome new employees at company level, communicate vision and culture
- **New Employee**: Actively participate in process, ask questions, seek feedback, engage with team and culture

- IT provisioning automation (user accounts, access permissions)
- Equipment tracking and inventory management
- Training completion tracking and reminders
- Buddy/mentor matching based on skills and interests
- Automated scheduling of orientation and meetings
- Progress tracking dashboards for onboarding milestones
- Feedback collection and analysis for process improvement
- Integration with HRIS for seamless data flow
- Automated welcome communications and resource delivery

## Purpose
To systematically identify, document, evaluate, and track automation opportunities that can improve efficiency, reduce manual effort, enhance accuracy, and free up human resources for higher-value work.

## How to Use This Document
1. **Submit Ideas**: Use the submission guidelines to add new automation ideas
2. **Review Existing Ideas**: Browse current opportunities for inspiration or duplication
3. **Track Progress**: Monitor the status of ideas through the evaluation pipeline
4. **Learn from Implementation**: Review completed automations for best practices
5. **Prioritize Work**: Use evaluation criteria to help prioritize automation efforts

## Submission Guidelines
When submitting an automation idea, please include:
- **Idea Title**: Clear, descriptive title
- **Submitted By**: Your name and department
- **Date Submitted**: Date of submission
- **Problem Statement**: Clear description of the current manual process or pain point
- **Proposed Solution**: Brief description of how automation could address the issue
- **Potential Benefits**: Estimated time savings, error reduction, cost savings, etc.
- **Estimated Effort**: Rough estimate of implementation complexity (Low/Medium/High)
- **Dependencies**: Any systems, data, or approvals needed
- **Stakeholders**: Who would be impacted or need to be involved
- **Priority**: Suggested priority level based on impact/effort

## Evaluation Criteria
Ideas are evaluated based on:
- **Impact Potential**: Time savings, cost reduction, error reduction, scalability
- **Implementation Effort**: Technical complexity, resource requirements, dependencies
- **Risk Assessment**: Security, compliance, change management, disruption potential
- **Strategic Alignment**: Alignment with organizational goals and priorities
- **Return on Investment**: Estimated ROI and payback period
- **Scalability**: Potential for reuse across teams or departments
- **User Experience**: Impact on employee and customer experience

## Evaluation Process
1. **Submission**: Idea submitted via this document or intake form
2. **Initial Review**: Automation team reviews for completeness and feasibility (within 1 week)
3. **Preliminary Assessment**: Initial scoring based on impact/effort matrix (within 2 weeks)
4. **Deep Dive**: Detailed analysis including technical feasibility, ROI calculation, risk assessment (within 4 weeks)
5. **Recommendation**: Go/no-go decision with recommended approach and timeline
6. **Implementation**: If approved, follows standard project intake and development process
7. **Review**: Post-implementation review to measure actual vs. expected benefits

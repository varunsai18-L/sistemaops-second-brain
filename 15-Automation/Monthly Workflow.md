# Monthly Workflow

## Purpose
Establish consistent monthly routines that support strategic planning, performance review, resource planning, and organizational alignment while ensuring important monthly deliverables are completed.

## Purpose
To provide a structured monthly rhythm that ensures strategic alignment, performance evaluation, resource allocation, and comprehensive reporting while maintaining operational continuity.

## Trigger
This workflow is triggered at the beginning of each month (typically the first business day) or at the end of the previous month (last business day).

## Workflow Steps
### Monthly Planning (First Few Days of Month)
1. **Review Previous Month Performance** (2-4 hours)
   - Review achievement of monthly goals and objectives
   - Analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics
   - Review financial performance against budget
   - Assess project and initiative progress
   - Identify successes, challenges, and lessons learned

2. **Strategic Review and Alignment** (2-3 hours)
   - Review progress toward quarterly and annual goals
   - Assess alignment with organizational strategy
   - Identify any needed strategic adjustments
   - Review market conditions and competitive landscape
   - Update risk assessments and mitigation plans

3. **Resource Planning and Allocation** (2-4 hours)
   - Review resource utilization from previous month
   - Plan resource allocation for upcoming month
   - Identify hiring needs or skill gaps
   - Plan for time off and resource availability
   - Review budget allocations and expenditures

4. **Goal Setting and Planning** (3-5 hours)
   - Establish specific, measurable goals for the month
   - Align team and individual goals with organizational objectives
   - Plan key initiatives and projects for the month
   - Identify critical milestones and deliverables
   - Develop contingency plans for known risks

### Ongoing Monthly Activities (Throughout Month)
1. **Weekly Integration**
   - Integrate monthly goals into weekly planning processes
   - Adjust weekly priorities based on monthly objectives
   - Ensure weekly activities support monthly goals

2. **Mid-Month Review** (Around day 15)
   - Review progress toward monthly goals
   - Identify any deviations from plan
   - Make adjustments to stay on track
   - Address emerging issues or opportunities
   - Communicate status to stakeholders

3. **Ongoing Reporting and Communication**
   - Provide regular updates to stakeholders
   - Maintain project and initiative status reporting
   - Share relevant metrics and KPI updates
   - Escalate issues requiring attention

### Monthly Closeout (Last Few Days of Month)
1. **Final Progress Review** (2-3 hours)
   - Review final progress against monthly goals
   - Document achievements and shortfalls
   - Analyze root causes for any missed targets
   - Capture lessons learned and best practices

2. **Comprehensive Reporting** (4-6 hours)
   - Prepare monthly performance reports
   - Update financial statements and budgets
   - Compile project and initiative status reports
   - Prepare KPI dashboard and trend analysis
   - Create executive summary for leadership

3. **Knowledge Capture and Sharing** (2-3 hours)
   - Document lessons learned and process improvements
   - Update knowledge base with new insights
   - Share success stories and best practices
   - Archive completed work appropriately

4. **Preparation for Next Month** (2-4 hours)
   - Preliminary planning for next month's priorities
   - Identify resource needs and constraints
   - Prepare for upcoming deadlines and commitments
   - Align with any known organizational changes

## Inputs
- Previous month's performance data and metrics
- Financial statements and budget reports
- Project and initiative status reports
- KPIs and performance dashboards
- Stakeholder feedback and requirements
- Market and competitive intelligence
- Organizational strategy and goals
- Resource availability and capacity reports
- Risk and issue logs

## Outputs
- Monthly performance report
- Updated financial statements and forecasts
- KPI dashboard and trend analysis
- Project and initiative status reports
- Lessons learned and best practices document
- Updated project documentation
- Resource plan for upcoming month
- Goals and objectives for upcoming month
- Risk assessment and mitigation plan
- Executive summary for leadership review

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Individual Contributors**: Provide accurate progress updates, contribute to reports, participate in planning sessions
- **Team Leads/Managers**: Consolidate team reports, facilitate planning sessions, ensure accurate reporting
- **Project Managers**: Provide detailed project status, update timelines and budgets, manage risks
- **Finance Team**: Prepare financial reports, analyze budget variances, provide financial insights
- **HR/Talent**: Provide workforce analytics, support planning efforts
- **Executive Leadership**: Review monthly performance, provide strategic direction, approve resource allocations
- **PMO/Operations**: Consolidate reports, ensure consistency, facilitate planning processes

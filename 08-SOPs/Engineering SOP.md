# Purpose
To establish standardized procedures for all engineering activities, ensuring consistency, quality, safety, and compliance with industry standards and organizational policies across all engineering disciplines.

# Scope
This SOP applies to all engineering teams, including but not limited to software, hardware, mechanical, electrical, and civil engineering. It covers design, development, testing, documentation, and release processes. It does not cover administrative functions handled by other departments (e.g., HR, Finance).

# Roles and Responsibilities
- **Engineering Manager**: Oversees implementation of this SOP, ensures team compliance, and allocates resources.
- **Team Leads**: Responsible for training team members on this SOP, monitoring adherence, and reporting issues.
- **Engineers**: Must follow this SOP in all engineering tasks, document deviations, and suggest improvements.
- **Quality Assurance Engineer**: Audits engineering processes for compliance and maintains quality records.
- **Safety Officer**: Ensures safety protocols are integrated into engineering workflows.

# Prerequisites
- Access to engineering tools, software, and equipment as required.
- Completion of mandatory safety and compliance training.
- Access to relevant technical standards (e.g., ISO, IEEE, ANSI) and organizational design guidelines.
- Approved project charter or work order.

# Procedure
1. **Project Initiation**
   - Receive and review project requirements.
   - Conduct feasibility study and risk assessment.
   - Obtain necessary approvals before proceeding.

2. **Design Phase**
   - Create detailed designs using approved tools and templates.
   - Perform design reviews and incorporate feedback.
   - Ensure designs comply with applicable standards and regulations.

3. **Development Phase**
   - Implement designs following coding, fabrication, or construction standards.
   - Conduct unit tests or prototype evaluations.
   - Maintain version control and change documentation.

4. **Testing and Validation**
   - Develop test plans based on requirements and designs.
   - Execute tests, record results, and identify defects.
   - Obtain sign-off from QA and stakeholders before proceeding.

5. **Release and Deployment**
   - Prepare release documentation and materials.
   - Conduct deployment or installation procedures.
   - Provide user training and support as needed.

6. **Post-Implementation Review**
   - Gather feedback and performance data.
   - Document lessons learned and update relevant SOPs.
   - Archive project records per retention policy.

# Checklist
- [ ] Project requirements documented and approved.
- [ ] Design reviews completed and signed off.
- [ ] Test plans approved and executed.
- [ ] Defects tracked and resolved.
- [ ] Release materials prepared and verified.
- [ ] Post-implementation review conducted.
- [ ] All records stored in designated repository.

# Exceptions
Any deviation from this SOP must be documented in a Deviation Request Form, approved by the Engineering Manager and Quality Assurance, and tracked until resolution. Emergency deviations require verbal approval followed by formal documentation within 24 hours.

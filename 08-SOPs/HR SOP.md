# Purpose
To establish standardized procedures for all human resources activities, ensuring consistency, compliance with labor laws, fair treatment of employees, and alignment with organizational goals in recruitment, onboarding, performance management, compensation, employee relations, and termination processes.

# Scope
This SOP applies to all HR professionals, managers, and employees involved in HR-related processes. It covers the entire employee lifecycle from recruitment to separation. It does not cover functional or technical job-specific procedures, which are handled by respective departments.

# Roles and Responsibilities
- **HR Director**: Oversees the HR function, ensures compliance with this SOP and legal requirements, and aligns HR strategy with business goals.
- **HR Managers/Generalists**: Responsible for executing HR processes, providing guidance to employees and managers, and maintaining HR records.
- **Recruiters**: Manage the hiring process, from sourcing to onboarding, ensuring a positive candidate experience.
- **Managers/Supervisors**: Responsible for providing accurate information for HR processes, conducting performance reviews, and addressing employee concerns in consultation with HR.
- **Employees**: Responsible for providing accurate personal information, adhering to company policies, and participating in HR processes (e.g., performance reviews, training).

# Prerequisites
- Access to HRIS (Human Resources Information System) and other HR tools.
- Knowledge of applicable labor laws and regulations (e.g., FMLA, ADA, EEOC).
- Approved job requisitions and budget.
- Availability: Optional.

# Procedure
1. **Recruitment and Hiring**
   - Receive and approve job requisition.
   - Develop job description and post on approved channels.
   - Screen resumes, conduct interviews, and perform background checks.
   - Extend job offer and complete new hire paperwork.

2. **Onboarding**
   - Prepare workspace and equipment.
   - Conduct orientation sessions (company policies, benefits, safety).
   - Assign mentor/buddy and schedule initial training.
   - Complete I-9 and other required documentation.

3. **Employee Data Management**
   - Maintain accurate and confidential employee records in HRIS.
   - Process changes (e.g., address, marital status, dependents) upon request.
   - Ensure compliance with data retention and privacy regulations.

4. **Performance Management**
   - Distribute performance review forms and guidelines.
   - Facilitate goal-setting sessions between employees and managers.
   - Collect and review performance evaluations.
   - Link outcomes to compensation, development, and retention actions.

5. **Compensation and Benefits**
   - Administer payroll processes accurately and timely.
   - Manage open enrollment and conduct annual reviews.
   - Manage benefits enrollment, changes, and inquiries.
   - Process bonuses, commissions, and other compensation components.

6. **Employee Relations**
   - Address employee concerns and complaints per
   - Conduct investigations into policy violations or complaints.
   - Facilitate conflict resolution and mediation when appropriate.
   - Ensure compliance with disciplinary and grievance procedures.

7. **Training and Development**
   - Identify training needs through performance reviews and organizational goals.
   - Coordinate internal and external training programs.
   - Track employee participation and maintain training records.
   - Evaluate training effectiveness and ROI.

8. **Offboarding and Separation**
   - Process resignation or termination notices.
   - Conduct exit interviews and collect feedback.
   - Retrieve company property and revoke system access.
   - Process final paycheck and benefits continuation (COBRA).

# Checklist
- [ ] Job requisition approved before recruitment begins.
- [ ] Background checks completed and cleared for new hires.
- [ ] New hire paperwork completed within first 3 days.
- [ ] Performance reviews completed annually per schedule.
- [ ] Payroll processed accurately and on time.
- [ ] Benefits administered per plan documents and regulations.
- [ ] Employee records maintained confidentially and securely.
- [ ] Exit interviews conducted for all voluntary separations.
- [ ] Final paycheck issued per state laws and company policy.

# Exceptions
Exceptions to HR SOPs must be documented, approved by the HR Director and Legal Counsel (if applicable), and aligned with employment laws and company policies. Emergency situations (e.g., sudden termination for safety reasons) require immediate action followed by thorough documentation and review.

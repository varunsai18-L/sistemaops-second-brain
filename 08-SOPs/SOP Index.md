# Purpose
This document serves as the central index for all Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) within the organization, providing easy access to engineering, human resources, and department-specific Operating Procedures (SOPs) within the organization, providing easy access to engineering, human resources, and department-specific operational guidelines.

# Scope
This index applies to all employees and contractors who are required to follow established SOPs for their respective functions. It serves as a central repository and navigation tool for finding relevant SOPs. It does not contain the detailed procedures themselves but points to where they can be found.

# Roles and Responsibilities
- **Document Control Owner**: Responsible for maintaining this index, ensuring it is up-to-date, and controlling access.
- **Department Heads**: Responsible for ensuring SOPs for their department are created, reviewed, and added to this index.
- **All Employees**: Responsible for consulting this index to find and follow the appropriate SOPs for their work.

# Prerequisites
- Access to the organizational knowledge base or document management system where SOPs are stored.
- Basic understanding of the organizational structure to navigate to the relevant department SOPs.

# Procedure
1. To find an SOP, first identify the relevant department or function (e.g., Engineering, HR, IT).
2. Locate the department-specific section in this index or navigate directly to the department's folder.
3. Open the specific SOP document from the list provided.
4. Review the SOP and follow the procedures as outlined.
5. If an SOP is missing or outdated, notify the Document Control Owner or your Department Head.

# Checklist
- [ ] Verify the SOP is the current version by checking the revision history.
- [ ] Ensure the SOP applies to your specific task or role.
- [ ] Contact the document owner if any steps are unclear.
- [ ] Report any discrepancies or suggestions for improvement to the Document Control Owner.

# Exceptions
If a specific task is not covered by an existing SOP, consult your supervisor or the Document Control Owner to determine if a new SOP needs to be created or if an alternative procedure should be followed. Emergency situations may require deviation from SOPs; such deviations must be documented and reviewed afterward.

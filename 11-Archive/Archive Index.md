## Archive Criteria

Items are moved to the archive based on the following criteria:
- Projects marked as completed or cancelled for over 6 months
- Clients with no active engagements for over 12 months
- Products that have been discontinued or superseded
- Meeting records older than 24 months
- Research materials superseded by newer studies
- Documentation superseded by current versions

Items in the archive are retained for historical reference and compliance purposes but are not actively maintained.

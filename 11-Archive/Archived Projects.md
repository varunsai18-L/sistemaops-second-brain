## Project Archive Criteria

Projects are moved to this archive when they meet the following criteria:
- Marked as completed or cancelled for over 6 months
- No active tasks or deliverables pending
- All deliverables have been accepted and closed out
- Lessons learned have been extracted and documented in the appropriate knowledge base areas

For detailed criteria, see [[Archive Policy]].

## How to Use This Index
Each archived project has its own dedicated page containing:
- Project charter and objectives
- Timeline and milestones
- Deliverables and outcomes
- Lessons learned
- Related documentation and artifacts
- Financial summary
- Stakeholder information

To restore an archived project to active status, follow the project reactivation process outlined in the [[Project Management Handbook]].

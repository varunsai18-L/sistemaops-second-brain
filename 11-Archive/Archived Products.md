## Product Archive Criteria

Products are moved to this archive when they meet the following criteria:
- Product has been discontinued or superseded by a newer version
- No active support contracts or maintenance agreements
- All known issues have been documented and archived
- Final product documentation preserved
- No active development or enhancement plans

For detailed criteria, see [[Archive Policy]].

## How to Use This Index
Each archived product has its own dedicated page containing:
- Product overview and features
- Release history and version information
- Technical specifications
- User documentation and guides
- Known issues and limitations
- Support information (where applicable)
- Migration guides to successor products

For information on migrating from an archived product to a current offering, see the [[Migration Guide]] in the appropriate product area.

## Meeting Archive Criteria

Meeting records are moved to this archive when:
- Meeting occurred more than 2 years ago (for most meeting types)
- All action items have been completed or deemed irrelevant
- Meeting records have been distilled into actionable decisions or project updates
- Recordings and transcripts have been archived per retention policy
- Meeting minutes have been formally approved and distributed

For detailed criteria, see [[Archive Policy]].

## How to Use This Index
Each archived meeting category contains individual meeting records including:
- Meeting agenda and objectives
- Attendee list
- Meeting minutes and decisions
- Action items and assignments
- Supporting documents and presentations
- Recordings and transcripts (where available and permitted)

Meeting archives are searchable by date, participant, topic, and outcome.

## Client Archive Criteria

Client relationships are moved to this archive when they meet the following criteria:
- No active engagements for over 12 months
- No pending contracts or statements of work
- All outstanding invoices settled
- Final project documentation delivered and accepted
- No pending renewals or extension discussions

For detailed criteria, see [[Archive Policy]].

## How to Use This Index
Each archived client has its own dedicated page containing:
- Client profile and contact information
- Engagement history
- Projects delivered
- Financial summary
- Key contacts and stakeholders
- Lessons learned from the relationship
- Contracts and agreements (where permitted)

To reactivate an archived client relationship, follow the client re-engagement process outlined in the [[Client Management Handbook]].

## Research Archive Criteria

Research materials are moved to this archive when:
- Research is more than 18 months old (unless identified as foundational)
- Findings have been incorporated into strategic plans or product roadmaps
- Underlying data has been archived per data retention policies
- Research objectives have been met or superseded
- Follow-up research has been initiated or deemed unnecessary

For detailed criteria, see [[Archive Policy]].

## How to Use This Index
Each archived research item includes:
- Research objective and methodology
- Executive summary of findings
- Detailed findings and analysis
- Data sources and methodology
- Limitations and assumptions
- Recommendations derived from research
- Raw data and analysis files (where permitted)
- Related presentations and reports

Archived research remains accessible for reference and historical context, but may not reflect current market conditions or technological landscapes.

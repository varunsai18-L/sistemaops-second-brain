# Archive Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines the criteria and procedures for moving items to the archive, ensuring that active knowledge remains current while preserving historical information for reference, compliance, and organizational memory.

## Scope
This policy applies to all items in the knowledge base, including projects, clients, products, meetings, research, and documentation.

## Archive Criteria

### Projects
- Marked as completed or cancelled for over 6 months
- No active tasks or deliverables pending
- All deliverables have been accepted and closed out
- Lessons learned have been extracted and documented

### Clients
- No active engagements for over 12 months
- No pending contracts or statements of work
- All outstanding invoices settled
- Final project documentation delivered and accepted

### Products
- Product has been discontinued or superseded by a newer version
- No active support contracts or maintenance agreements
- All known issues have been documented and archived
- Final product documentation preserved

### Meetings
- Meeting records older than 24 months
- Action items from the meeting have been completed or superseded
- Meeting minutes have been distilled into actionable items or decisions
- Recording and transcripts no longer needed for active reference

### Research
- Research materials superseded by newer studies or data
- Research project completed and findings published or archived
- Raw data no longer needed for active analysis
- Literature reviews updated with more recent sources

### Documentation
- Documentation superseded by current versions
- Process documentation for obsolete processes
- Reference materials for deprecated tools or technologies
- Legal or compliance documents superseded by newer versions

## Archive Process

1. **Identification**: Items meeting archive criteria are identified by their owners or through periodic review.
2. **Review**: Proposed archival items are reviewed by the knowledge steward or designated reviewer.
3. **Approval**: Items are approved for archival by the appropriate authority (project manager, account manager, product owner, etc.).
4. **Movement**: Approved items are moved to the appropriate archive folder.
5. **Notification**: Relevant stakeholders are notified of the archival.
6. **Retention**: Archived items are retained according to organizational retention policies and compliance requirements.

## Retrieval
Archived items remain accessible for reference, audit, and compliance purposes. Users can search and retrieve archived items through the knowledge base search functionality.

## Review Schedule
This policy is reviewed annually or as needed to ensure it remains aligned with organizational needs and compliance requirements.

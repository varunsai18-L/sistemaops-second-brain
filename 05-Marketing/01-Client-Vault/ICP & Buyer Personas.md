# 🎯 Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Buyer Personas

> **Pillar 1:** Precise definitions of target buyer personas, organizational profiles, decision drivers, and buying triggers.

---

## 🏢 Target Organization Profile (Company ICP)

| Attribute | Target Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Industry** | Software Services, Defense Tech, Enterprise IT, Engineering Consultancies |
| **Company Size** | 50 to 500+ employees |
| **Tech Stack** | Uses Odoo, openDesk / XWiki, GitLab/GitHub, Python/TypeScript |
| **Monthly Volume** | 10 to 50+ RFQs / Enterprise Proposals per month |
| **Core Challenge** | Manual RFQ scoping, uncoordinated team allocation, architecture compliance risk |

---

## 👤 Primary Persona 1: The Executive (CEO / Managing Director)
- **Role:** Chief Executive Officer, Managing Director, Founder
- **Goals:** Increase RFQ win rates, maximize gross margins, eliminate project delivery failures.
- **Fear / Risk:** Committing to an unviable RFQ that leads to financial loss or client reputation damage.
- **Message Hook:** *"Turn incoming RFQs into instant GO/NO-GO executive decisions backed by real-time team capacity and risk scoring."*

---

## 👤 Primary Persona 2: The Technical Lead (CTO / VP of Engineering)
- **Role:** Chief Technology Officer, VP of Engineering, Solutions Architect
- **Goals:** Ensure code/architecture standards, match qualified developers to stack requirements, prevent developer burnout.
- **Fear / Risk:** Assigning developers without required technical skills or over-allocating capacity.
- **Message Hook:** *"Automate skill matching across 60+ engineers and 380+ technical docs in seconds."*

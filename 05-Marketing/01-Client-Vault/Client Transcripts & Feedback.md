# 🗣️ Client Transcripts & Raw Feedback Repository

> **Pillar 1:** Centralized repository of client meeting transcripts, raw feedback, recorded objections, and quotes to fuel marketing messaging.

---

## 🎙️ Executive Quotes & Raw Transcripts

### Client A (CTO, Enterprise Logistics)
> *"Our biggest bottleneck is evaluating 20+ RFQs every month. We don't know if our engineering team has the bandwidth or exact skill set until weeks into the evaluation cycle."*

**Key Takeaways for Marketing:**
- Pain Point: Delayed RFQ scoping & skill/capacity uncertainty.
- Desired State: Instant skill-capacity matching and 5-dimension risk scoring.

---

### Client B (VP of Engineering, Defense Tech)
> *"We use Odoo and openDesk internally, but the data lives in silos. If an AI tool could read our documentation and match active employee capacity to new proposals, it would save us hundreds of engineering hours."*

**Key Takeaways for Marketing:**
- Pain Point: Siloed data across Odoo HR, Odoo Tasks, and openDesk XWiki.
- Value Proposition: Closed-loop Second Brain integration with PVMS 2.0.

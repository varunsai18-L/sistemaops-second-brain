- **Body:**

  Most software engineering teams take 10 to 14 days to evaluate complex RFQs—only to discover mid-project that key developers are over-allocated or missing specific tech stack skills.
  
  At SystemaOps, we built a **Second Brain RFQ Engine (PVMS 2.0)** that connects Odoo HR employee workloads and openDesk XWiki technical documentation to evaluate proposals in under 60 seconds.
  
  Would you be open to seeing a 2-minute interactive demo of how this generates instant GO / NO-GO recommendations?
  
  Best regards,  
  **Varun & SystemaOps Growth Team**

# Team Meetings

## Overview
Central repository for all team meeting notes, agendas, and action items. This includes meetings for all departments and cross-functional teams.

## Team Meeting Structure

### Engineering Team
- **Weekly Sprint Planning**: Mondays at 9:00 AM
- **Daily Stand-ups**: Monday-Friday at 9:15 AM
- **Sprint Review/Demo**: Fridays at 4:00 PM
- **Retrospective**: Fridays at 4:45 PM
- **Architecture Review**: Bi-weekly Wednesdays at 2:00 PM

### Marketing Team
- **Weekly Campaign Planning**: Tuesdays at 10:00 AM
- **Content Review**: Wednesdays at 11:00 AM
- **Analytics Review**: Thursdays at 3:00 PM
- **Brand Strategy**: Bi-weekly Mondays at 2:00 PM

### Sales Team
- **Weekly Pipeline Review**: Mondays at 11:00 AM
- **Sales Training**: Tuesdays at 2:00 PM
- **Client Review**: Wednesdays at 10:00 AM
- **Strategy Session**: Thursdays at 4:00 PM

### Operations Team
- **Daily Ops Check-in**: 8:45 AM daily
- **Weekly Process Review**: Mondays at 3:00 PM
- **Vendor Management**: Tuesdays at 10:00 AM
- **Capacity Planning**: Monthly first Thursday

### Product Team
- **Product Sync**: Tuesdays at 9:00 AM
- **User Research Review**: Wednesdays at 1:00 PM
- **Roadmap Planning**: Bi-weekly Thursdays at 10:00 AM
- **UX Review**: Fridays at 11:00 AM

## Cross-Functional Teams
- **Product Launch Task Force**: As needed
- **Customer Escalation Task Force**: As needed
- **Innovation Committee**: Quarterly
- **Culture Committee**: Monthly

## Meeting Templates
See [[Meeting Template]] for standardized meeting formats.

## Action Item Tracking
All action items from team meetings should be tracked in [[Action Items]] with:
- Clear owner
- Due date
- Status (To Do, In Progress, Done)
- Related project/initiative

## Meeting Archives
Past meeting notes are archived by team and date:
- `/Meetings/Team Meetings/Engineering/YYYY/MM/`
- `/Meetings/Team Meetings/Marketing/YYYY/MM/`
- etc.

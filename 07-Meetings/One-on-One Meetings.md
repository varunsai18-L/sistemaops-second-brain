# One-on-One Meetings

## Overview
Central repository for all one-on-one meeting notes between managers and direct reports, including performance discussions, career development, and regular check-ins.

## One-on-One Meeting Structure

### Manager to Direct Report
- **Frequency**: Weekly or bi-weekly (30-60 minutes)
- **Purpose**: 
  - Check-in on progress and wellbeing
  - Provide feedback and coaching
  - Discuss career development and goals
  - Address blockers and challenges
  - Align on priorities and expectations

### Skip-level Meetings
- **Frequency**: Monthly or quarterly
- **Participants**: Manager's manager and direct report
- **Purpose**:
  - Gain deeper insights into team dynamics
  - Identify organizational issues
  - Provide alternative feedback channel
  - Strengthen cross-level relationships

### Peer One-on-Ones
- **Frequency**: As needed or monthly
- **Participants**: Colleagues at similar levels
- **Purpose**:
  - Collaboration improvement
  - Knowledge sharing
  - Conflict resolution
  - Networking and relationship building

### New Employee One-on-Ones
- **Frequency**: Weekly for first month, then bi-weekly
- **Purpose**:
  - Onboarding progress check
  - Question and concern resolution
  - Cultural integration assessment
  - Early performance feedback

## One-on-One Meeting Best Practices

### For Managers
1. **Prepare**: Review previous notes, current projects, and any concerns
2. **Listen First**: Start by asking how they're doing and actively listen
3. **Focus on Development**: Discuss growth, skills, and career aspirations
4. **Provide Feedback**: Give specific, timely, and balanced feedback
5. **Remove Blockers**: Identify and help resolve obstacles
6. **Set Clear Actions**: End with agreed-upon next steps and follow-ups
7. **Document**: Take notes and share summary after meeting

### For Direct Reports
1. **Come Prepared**: Bring updates, questions, and discussion topics
2. **Be Honest**: Share both successes and challenges
3. **Seek Feedback**: Ask for specific feedback on performance
4. **Discuss Goals**: Talk about career aspirations and development needs
5. **Follow Up**: Complete action items and prepare for next meeting

## Meeting Agenda Template
1. **Check-in** (5 min): How are you doing personally and professionally?
2. **Progress Review** (10 min): What's been accomplished since last meeting?
3. **Challenges & Blockers** (10 min): What obstacles are you facing?
4. **Feedback & Coaching** (10 min): Manager feedback and employee questions
5. **Career Development** (10 min): Goals, skills, growth opportunities
6. **Action Items & Next Steps** (5 min): Agree on follow-ups and deadlines

## One-on-One Meeting Archives
One-on-one meeting notes are organized by employee:
- `/Meetings/One-on-One Meetings/[Employee Name]/YYYY/MM/`
- Each employee folder contains:
  - Meeting notes by date
  - Goal tracking and progress
  - Feedback history
  - Development plans

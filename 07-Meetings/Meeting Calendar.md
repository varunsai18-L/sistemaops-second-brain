- Team Stand-ups (Daily/Weekly)
- Project Updates (Weekly/Bi-weekly)
- Client Syncs (Weekly/Monthly)
- Executive Leadership (Weekly)
- All-Hands Meetings (Monthly)
- Board Meetings (Quarterly)

## Calendar Integration
- Google Calendar Integration
- Outlook Calendar Sync
- iCal Export Options

## Upcoming Key Dates
- **Weekly Team Stand-ups**: Every Monday, 9:00 AM
- **Project Review Meetings**: Every Wednesday, 2:00 PM
- **Client Sync Meetings**: Every Thursday, 10:00 AM
- **Executive Leadership**: Every Friday, 4:00 PM
- **All-Hands Meeting**: First Friday of each month, 10:00 AM
- **Board Meeting**: Quarterly, Third Thursday

## Calendar Filters
- By Team: Engineering, Marketing, Sales, Operations
- By Meeting Type: Stand-up, Review, Planning, Retrospective
- By Attendee Type: Internal, Client, Stakeholder
- By Priority: High, Medium, Low

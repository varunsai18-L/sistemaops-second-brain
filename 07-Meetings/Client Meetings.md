# Client Meetings

## Overview
Central repository for all client meeting notes, agendas, action items, and follow-ups. Includes meetings with prospects, active clients, and strategic partners.

## Client Meeting Structure

### Prospect/Initial Meetings
- **Initial Discovery Call**: 60 minutes
  - Understand client needs and challenges
  - Present company capabilities
  - Define next steps
- **Technical Deep Dive**: 90 minutes
  - Technical requirements gathering
  - Solution architecture discussion
  - Proof of concept planning
- **Proposal Review**: 60 minutes
  - Review proposed solution
  - Address questions/concerns
  - Negotiation preparation

### Active Client Meetings
- **Kickoff Meeting**: 90 minutes (project start)
  - Introduce project team
  - Review project scope and timeline
  - Establish communication protocols
- **Weekly Status Updates**: 30-60 minutes
  - Progress review
  - Issue identification and resolution
  - Upcoming milestones
- **Milestone Reviews**: 60-90 minutes (at key milestones)
  - Deliverable review
  - Client feedback collection
  - Next phase planning
- **Executive Briefings**: 30-45 minutes (monthly/quarterly)
  - Executive summary of progress
  - Strategic alignment discussion
  - Executive sponsor updates
- **Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR)**: 60-90 minutes
  - Business impact review
  - ROI analysis
  - Strategic planning for next quarter
- **Retrospective/Lessons Learned**: 60 minutes (project end)
  - Project retrospective
  - Lessons learned documentation
  - Future opportunity identification

### Strategic Partner Meetings
- **Partner Alignment**: Monthly
  - Joint go-to-market planning
  - Co-marketing initiatives
  - Technical integration planning
- **Joint Client Meetings**: As needed
  - Coordinated client presentations
  - Joint solution demonstrations
  - Collaborative problem-solving

## Client Meeting Best Practices

### Preparation
1. Review client history and previous meeting notes
2. Prepare agenda and share in advance (24 hours minimum)
3. Prepare relevant materials (slides, demos, documents)
4. Identify meeting objectives and desired outcomes
5. Prepare for potential questions/objections

### During Meeting
1. Start on time, review agenda and objectives
2. Take detailed notes (assign a note-taker if needed)
3. Capture all action items with owners and due dates
4. Confirm understanding and next steps before ending
5. Send meeting summary within 24 hours

### Follow-up
1. Distribute meeting notes and action items to all attendees
2. Update CRM with meeting outcomes and next steps
3. Complete assigned action items before next meeting
4. Proactively communicate any delays or issues
5. Schedule next meeting before concluding current one

## Key Responsibilities
### Talent Acquisition
- Sourcing and interviewing candidates
- Managing the recruitment lifecycle
- Employer branding and candidate experience
- Campus recruitment and internships
- Executive search and succession planning

### Employee Relations
- Performance management processes
- Employee relations and conflict resolution
- Policy development and administration
- Leave management (FMLA, etc.)
- Workplace investigations

### Learning & Development
- Training needs assessment
- Leadership development programs
- Skills training and workshops
- Tuition reimbursement programs
- Onboarding and orientation

### Compensation & Benefits
- Salary benchmarking and equity planning
- Benefits program design and administration
- Payroll processing oversight
- Wellness program management
- Recognition and rewards programs

### HR Operations
- HRIS management (Workday, BambooHR, etc.)
- Employee data management and reporting
- HR compliance (EEO, OSHA, etc.)
- Document and record management
- HR metrics and analytics

## Programs & Policies
- **Onboarding Program**: New hire orientation and assimilation
- **Performance Management**: Quarterly reviews and goal setting
- **Learning Stipend**: Annual budget for professional development
- **Wellness Program**: Mental health, fitness, and preventive care
- **Parental Leave**: Enhanced leave policies beyond statutory
- **Referral Program**: Employee referral bonuses
- **Diversity Initiatives**: ERGs, bias training, inclusive hiring

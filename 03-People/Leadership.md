## Leadership Principles
Our leadership team follows these core principles:
1. **Customer Obsession**: Start with the customer and work backwards
2. **Ownership**: Think long term and don't sacrifice long-term value for short-term results
3. **Invent and Simplify**: Expect and require innovation and invention from your team
4. **Are Right, A Lot**: Seek diverse perspectives and work to disconfirm your beliefs
5. **Learn and Be Curious**: Never stop learning and look for new possibilities
6. **Hire and Develop the Best**: Raise the performance bar with every hire and promotion
7. **Insist on the Highest Standards**: Have relentlessly high standards
8. **Think Big**: Thinking small is a self-fulfilling prophecy
9. **Bias for Action**: Speed matters in business
10. **Frugality**: Accomplish more with less
11. **Earn Trust**: Listen attentively, speak candidly, and treat others respectfully
12. **Dive Deep**: Operate at all levels, stay connected to the details, audit frequently
13. **Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit**: Respectfully challenge decisions when you disagree
14. **Deliver Results**: Focus on the key inputs for your business and deliver with the right quality
